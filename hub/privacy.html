Home Hub privacy policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

Home Hub is a personal wall display for one household. It isn't offered to the public, has no accounts, and has no other users.

What it accesses

With the household's permission, Home Hub reads its own Google Calendar feeds, Todoist lists, and Google Nest thermostat status (temperature, humidity, mode and setpoints), and can change thermostat setpoints when someone taps the controls on the display.

Where the data goes

Credentials and access tokens are stored only as encrypted secrets in the household's own Cloudflare Worker. Data is fetched on demand to draw the display and is not stored, logged, sold or shared with anyone. Nothing is used for advertising or analytics.

Turning it off

Access to Nest devices can be revoked at any time from the Google account's third-party connections settings, or by deleting the Worker's secrets.
